Overview

Perfect Internet was a focused three-month product and brand engagement where I was entrusted to shape the product experience from early brand direction through launch preparation. The work covered branding, look and feel, go-to-market strategy, UI/UX design, marketing direction, and front-end development.

My Role

I worked as the Product Designer across the core product and marketing touchpoints. My responsibility was to translate the business direction into a coherent brand experience, define the interface direction, support the go-to-market narrative, and build the front-end foundation needed to bring the experience to life.


Background

The project needed more than a surface-level visual refresh. Perfect Internet required a practical identity and product experience that could communicate clearly, support acquisition, and move quickly toward market validation.


The Challenge

The main challenge was aligning multiple tracks of work within a short timeline: brand definition, product interface design, marketing direction, and implementation. Each piece had to feel connected, but also be practical enough for a lean team to ship and iterate.

Workstreams

Branding & Look and Feel

Defined the early visual direction, tone, and design language so the product could feel credible and distinct from the first touchpoint.

UI/UX Design

Structured the product experience, mapped key user flows, and designed screens that could support the product's core value proposition.

GTM & Marketing

Helped shape the launch narrative, marketing direction, and positioning needed to introduce the product to its target users.
