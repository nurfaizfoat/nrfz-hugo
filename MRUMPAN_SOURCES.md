# MR. UMPAN case-study sources

Research checked on 13 October 2026. The story is editable in
`themes/neobrute/content/mrumpan_case_study.md`.

## First-person account

The owner supplied these facts: retrenchment during early COVID-19; school closures
and childcare while their partner worked as a frontliner; RM500 starting capital;
approximately RM55,000 **cumulative retail sales during 2020–2023**; declining sales
as routines and repayment commitments returned; closure in early 2025; best sellers
Dedak Rohu, Pengasah Mata Kail and Set Kombo Grease 3 IN 1; learning in GTM,
distribution, social media, editing and post-production.

The sales total is not independently audited, not profit, not MRR, and not a
2020–2025 lifetime total. Copy avoids invented customer counts, conversion rates,
market-share claims and descriptions of unconfirmed personal events. Statements
about the business slowdown are the owner's experience, not a causal economic
study.

## Malaysian repayment-relief context

- Ministry of Finance, 29 July 2020: automatic six-month moratorium from April
  2020, repayments resuming from 1 October for those able to pay, targeted relief:
  https://www.mof.gov.my/portal/ms/berita/siaran-media/lanjutan-moratorium-bantuan-bank-bersasar
- BNM, 30 September 2020: automatic moratorium ending on 30 September, followed
  by targeted repayment assistance rather than all relief ending simultaneously:
  https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/transition-to-targeted-repayment-assistance
- BNM, 6 July 2021: PEMULIH six-month opt-in moratorium; applications from 7 July
  2021; interest/profit continued to accrue on deferred payments:
  https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/six-mth-mora-begins-20210707
